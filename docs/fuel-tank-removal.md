# Fuel Tank Removal Notes

Composed by Cyclehead21@gmail.com

Rear of the car needs to be pretty high to allow room to maneuver the tank. It doesn’t come straight down (with AC and heater hose lines intact).

With ¼ tank of fuel, it is not that heavy.

Tank lips are secured with four 12 mm bolts.

## Hoses to the tank:

There are 5 hoses coming off the top of the tank that must be disconnected.

Two small hoses leave the top of the tank, and thread through the “hobbit hole” into the engine compartment.

One vent line attaches to the fuel pump plate.

Two plastic vent tubes attach to the charcoal canister.

One large rubber hose at the bottom aft edge of the fuel tank is the fuel filler tube.

Disconnect two small vent hoses by the forward engine mount. Two metal pinch clamps on the firewall adjacent to the forward engine mount (in the engine compartment)

Disconnect two plastic latch hoses from the charcoal canister (behind the driver’s seat). The large plastic latch stinks, I had to manually disconnect fingers with a small screwdriver.

Disconnect the plastic vent line at the fuel pump.

Disconnect large fuel filler at the upper hose clamp. (Moderate amount of fuel in the tank is okay. Just pop a plug into the open hose.)

Wrestle the tank down: slide it left as far as possible to get away from AC lines. Rotate rear edge downward to allow front lip to clear AC lines. Lower RH side of the tank. Then slide the tank right to clear the heater hose hard line on the left side.

Tank must be twisted to clear the hard lines. To reinstall I had to manhandle the tank RH side back up into position.

While the tank is out, it’s a good time to use a siphon to remove sand and chunks of rust from the fuel hopper.

Scrub tank sealing surfaces for fuel pump seal and vent seal using scotchbrite.
