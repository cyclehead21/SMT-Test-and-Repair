# Parking Brake Cables - Replace

## Without Dropping the Fuel Tank

Dec 2024

Composed by [Cyclehead21@gmail.com](mailto:Cyclehead21@gmail.com)

Feel free to copy and share, just give me a little credit!

## Summary

Cut off the forward ends of the old cables.

Pull them out of the car from below. Push a stiff wire into the hole from above. Attach the new cable to the stiff wire and pull it into position from above.

Or better yet - think ahead and use the old cable to pull a wire through. Attach the new cable and use the wire to pull them back into position.

## Details

This method took me about 2 hours.

It does not allow reinstalling the forwardmost cable clamp which is only accessible by lowering the fuel tank.

The two rear cable clamps can be installed, same as factory.

I don’t worry about the cable chafing on top of the fuel tank. The cable will only move when you use the parking brake. The outer cable is made from a spiral steel with a heavy plastic coating.

Leaving the fuel tank in place avoids disconnecting 8 attachments! (1 fuel line, 5 vapor lines, 2 electrical connectors) and loosening air conditioning refrigerant lines and coolant lines for access to remove the tank.

## Steps

1. Remove both seats. Four 14mm bolts for each.
2. Remove the plastic center console.
3. Cut off the forward tips of the parking brake cables using a 20,000rpm cutoff wheel. (Protect your carpet with an old towel so the sparks don’t melt the carpet)
4. Spray the cable surfaces with silicone to help them slide through the clamps when you remove them.
5. Disconnect the aft end of the cables from the brake calipers. Don’t lose the fancy little steel clips. Remove the clevis pins.
6. From below, unbolt the two cable clamps on each cable. One is hard to reach since it is bolted to the frame adjacent to the fuel tank. (12mm)
7. Pull the cables out of the car from below.
8. Go back on top and fish out the old rubber boots. They usually are stuck halfway in the oval sheetmetal hole.
9. Remove the forward clamp from the new cables before you start installing them. ( Leave the two rear clamps alone.). The steel strap can be bent and removed by hand. Cut off the steel band so it won’t rattle against the top of the fuel tank. I used a 20k rpm cutoff wheel. Be careful not to cut into the new cable!
10. Slide the new rubber boot (bellows) off the front of the cable.
11. Use a stiff wire to fish the new cable. Push the stiff wire into the hole from above. Check below to make sure it comes out on the side of the tank where it belongs. (Or use the wire you pulled through using the old cables)
12. Attach the new cable to the stiff wire. I used some bailing wire to attach the two together. Then wrapped the whole joint with blue tape to minimize snagging as I pull it through from above.
13. Pull the new cable into position from above. Unwrap the blue tape and the bailing wire.
14. Spray the cable and the new boot with silicone. Push the new boot over the cable and tuck it into position using your fingertips. It’s a little hard to reach inside the sheetmetal hole. (This will place the corrugated bellows forward of the bulkhead, instead of aft. But the boot works the same)
15. Go below and bolt the middle cable clamp into place. The bolt is hard to reach! You can remove the clamp on the coolant or refrigerant lines to make access a little easier.
16. Attach the aft clamp
17. Attach the aft end of the cable to the brake caliper.
18. Attach the forward ends of the cables to the parking brake lever.
19. Reassemble the console and seats.

For info, This is the forwardmost cable clamp (shown on a car where fuel tank has been removed). It is not accessible unless you remove the fuel tank. The rubber boot is to the left. This procedure will leave this one clamp in place, and empty (unused).
