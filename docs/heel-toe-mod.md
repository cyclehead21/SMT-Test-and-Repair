# Heel Toe Mod

Written by Cyclehead

If you like this mod, feel free to share

Just give me a little credit :)

![Heel Toe Mod photograph](../images/heel-toe-mod/image9.jpg)

This mod simplifies heel-and-toe driving. It allows you to modulate the throttle, while you have the brake pedal depressed - but without any silly ankle-twisting maneuvers. You simply rock your foot to the side, or wiggle your toes. (See the video below). It allows you to place your foot firmly on the brake pedal, and blip the throttle (which needs much less pressure) using your toes or VERY MINOR rocking of your ankle.

The goal is to be able to blip the throttle while you have the brake pedal depressed. The rest of the time you don’t want any interference. To do this, I added a short piece of aluminum pipe to the gas pedal. The critical feature: I positioned the surface of the pipe to be level with the brake pedal, while the brake pedal is depressed (braking). Note that the brake pedal height is adjustable.

When the brake pedal is even with the metal pipe (when the brake is depressed) you only have to wiggle your toes, or rock your foot SLIGHTLY to the side, to blip the throttle. It works well with shoes on, or barefoot. Just hang your foot a slightly off the brake pedal and rock it slightly to blip the throttle. This avoids the standard method of holding the brake depressed with your toe, while pressing the throttle with your heel - awkward and cumbersome!

The brake pedal height (when depressed) can be adjusted and fine tuned by loosening the jamb nut, and adjusting the length of the pushrod that goes into the clutch brake cylinder.

Another attempt using 1 ¼ OD thick wall aluminum pipe, cut in half.

It sticks out about 1.5 inches from the LH side of the gas pedal. On this one, I tapped the existing hole in the gas pedal rod to M5x0.8, mostly because I found a screw in my bucket of screws that size. But the long screw kept everything nice and tight, so the pedal doesn’t wobble. I drilled the plastic parts of the gas pedal to 4.5mm diameter, and let the screw cut its own threads in the plastic.
