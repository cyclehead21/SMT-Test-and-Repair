## VVTi Solenoid rattle

[https://youtube.com/shorts/48Hkj3pupf0?si=t20OsW33qUjkpfpf](https://youtube.com/shorts/48Hkj3pupf0?si=t20OsW33qUjkpfpf)

[https://youtube.com/shorts/B7M4GpQZqHg?si=HUwka04mx7H4458H](https://youtube.com/shorts/B7M4GpQZqHg?si=HUwka04mx7H4458H)

This was a very loud noise starting at 4200 rpm and up. It was so loud, it sounded like something hitting the sheetmetal firewall on the passenger side.

The noise went away after I removed and cleaned the VVTi solenoid (basically wiped it off with a rag). Later I replaced it with a new solenoid PN 15330-22030.

## Serpentine belt tensioner rattle

[https://youtube.com/shorts/ZluNuLYmjgU?si=-d-JRWuwdiYDQEqa](https://youtube.com/shorts/ZluNuLYmjgU?si=-d-JRWuwdiYDQEqa)

This was an intermittent rattle audible at idle, especially when the engine was warm. It sounded like a fast Morse-code tapping since it was not steady and uniform.

I replaced the metal belt idler PN 13570-22010, plastic tensioner idler pulley Dorman PN 419-615, alternator, VVTi solenoid PN 15330-22030, and Timing chain tensioner PN 13540-22022. Nothing made the noise change - yet the noise WAS gone when I removed the belt.

Finally I tried adding and removing tension in the idler arm (engine running) by pushing and pulling on a wrench with 19mm socket on the tensioner stud. Adding tension made no difference in the sound, but REMOVING tension (compressing the spring and making the belt loose) made the noise stop!

I believe the tensioner had some free motion at the end of its travel, allowing something to rattle. When I compressed the spring it took up the slack and stopped the noise.

Fix: replaced the tensioner PN 16620-22011
