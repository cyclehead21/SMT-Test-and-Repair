# SMT - Self Relearn Procedure

Composed by [Cyclehead21@gmail.com](mailto:Cyclehead21@gmail.com)

There is a “self relearn” procedure you can run without using techstream software. It is a “self test” mode. I don’t have success getting it to launch reliably. Sometimes it works, other times it wont.

The first step is to disconnect the battery for 30 minutes or longer - overnight (most reliable). Make sure everything is in neutral before you reconnect the battery. Look at the stub shaft on the transmission to confirm the hole is vertical, and roll the car to verify. Make certain the cabin floor shift lever is in neutral.

See: How to force neutral

[https://youtu.be/EIIB7LKWX6I?si=56GKKqGmKKF9W_RX](https://youtu.be/EIIB7LKWX6I?si=56GKKqGmKKF9W_RX)

Reconnect the battery and switch on ignition but don’t engage the starter (don’t start the engine). Wait 2-3 minutes. At some point, the car should begin cycling the clutch, and commence shifting all by itself, into each gear sequentially (6),5,4,3,2,1,R,N and stop with the green light illuminated. Gently rock the rear wheels (or the car) back and forth while it’s shifting gears. This will prevent the transmission getting “stuck on a gear tooth” (due to shifting while the car is stationary). If the gear countdown is successful, and it completes with a solid green light, then you’re all finished! If it completes with a flashing green light - then wait patiently for about 15 seconds longer and the green light should go solid. If not, you’ll need to retrieve the TCU error codes using techstream and the J2534 cable.

**Note:** The “self relearn” sequence appears to duplicate some of the “full relearn” processes. If it initiates and completes successfully, then I don’t believe that it is necessary to perform a “full relearn”.
