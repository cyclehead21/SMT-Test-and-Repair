Cheap bumper-cover mounted fog lights with glass “projector” lenses.

Difficulty about 5/10, requires some fabrication with simple hand tools, and some careful layout for cutting the bumper cover. After changing to LED bulbs and testing at night… I love these things!

## Steps

1. Remove the bumper cover
2. Find the “factory” fog light cutout marks inside the bumper
3. Center new cutouts
4. Trim off the mounting clips from the fog light bezels to make a flat surface for mounting
5. Rivet bezels to the bumper cover
6. Mount the light assemblies

## Installation details

Walmart.com (and eBay and CarID.com) has this cheap Chinese kit. Cheap relay, wiring and switch included. Also included are 55w H11 quartz bulbs. They use lots of power, very hot, and they shine yellow. Cheap LED replacement bulbs are much better.

![Fog light kit listing shown in the source document](../images/fog-light-mod/02-fog-light-kit.png)

¼ inch thick “expanded PVC” is easy to cut, drill and epoxy.

![Expanded PVC mounting plate with circular cutout](../images/fog-light-mod/03-expanded-pvc-mounting-plate.jpg)

The light assembly has no provisions for mounting in the car. I epoxied the flat face of this ring to a piece of expanded pvc.

![Light mounting ring bonded to the expanded PVC plate](../images/fog-light-mod/04-light-ring-bonded-to-pvc.jpg)

For simplicity laying out, I centered my cutout on the factory marks molded into the bumper cover. The molded lines are VERY light and hard to see. They were put there by Toyota to aid in locating the cuts for the TRD factory-option fog lights.

![Cutout layout marked inside the bumper cover](../images/fog-light-mod/05-bumper-cutout-layout.jpg)

You need a flat flange to mount the bezels. Cut off the “Camaro” mounting clips and flange.

![Fog light bezel and mounting clips before trimming](../images/fog-light-mod/06-bezel-mounting-clips.jpg)

Lay out a rivet hole pattern.

![Rivet hole pattern laid out on the bezel](../images/fog-light-mod/07-bezel-rivet-hole-pattern.jpg)

Make an accurate hole cutout template from the bezel. Cut the bumper carefully!

![Bumper cover opening being cut](../images/fog-light-mod/08-bumper-opening-cut.jpg)

The bezels need to be “level” so the light beam cutoff will be level. Not a big deal if you miss a little because you can enlarge the foglight adjustment slot later if needed.

![Bezel held level during layout](../images/fog-light-mod/09-bezel-leveling.jpg)

Drill thru bumper skin for pop rivets.

![Pop rivets positioned through the bezel and bumper cover](../images/fog-light-mod/10-pop-rivet-placement.jpg)

Use aluminum “backup washers” to keep plastic from splitting.

![Backup washers on the inside of the bumper cover](../images/fog-light-mod/11-rivet-backup-washers.jpg)

![Installed bezel viewed from the outside of the bumper](../images/fog-light-mod/12-installed-bezel.jpg)

## Mounting the light assemblies

I mounted the light assemblies to metal straps drilled thru the back of the crash bar.

![Mounting strap location behind the bumper cover](../images/fog-light-mod/13-mounting-strap-location.jpg)

Make your mounts prettier than mine if that’s important to you. I used scraps of metal straps from my metal bin.

![Light assembly mounted on metal straps](../images/fog-light-mod/14-light-assembly-metal-straps.jpg)

The light on the right side is very close to the washer reservoir.

![Right-side light clearance beside the washer reservoir](../images/fog-light-mod/15-washer-reservoir-clearance.jpg)

## Wiring

I ran wiring inside the crash bar, from right to left. The wires in the kit are about 4” too short. Splice them to extend.

![Wiring extension shown at the light assembly](../images/fog-light-mod/16-wiring-extension.jpg)

Pick up a power source in the frunk relay/fuse box. The little metal bar by the tip of my thumb is a +12V buss (the square hole adjacent to the green 30 amp fuse). I trimmed a blade connector to fit in the slot opening. I tucked a new fuse holder into the fuse block as well.

![Power connection in the frunk fuse and relay box](../images/fog-light-mod/17-fuse-box-power-connection.jpg)

For the foglight switch wire, I drilled one hole thru the firewall, just left of the clutch mounting bolt. (Hidden by the strut support). I mounted the relay just forward of the relay/fuse box. I tapped into a connector at driver’s kick panel for a wire that is only hot when parking lights are on. It will go thru the new foglight switch and trip the relay. You can see my homemade hot-wire connector in the photo below, in the bottom left corner of the fuse/relay box.

![Fog light relay and fuse box location in the frunk](../images/fog-light-mod/18-relay-and-fuse-box-location.jpg)

![Projector light installed in the bumper bezel](../images/fog-light-mod/19-installed-projector-light.jpg)

## LED bulbs and night-drive photos

I installed some cheap H11 LED bulbs to replace the ones that come in the kit. I suspect the kit’s wire harness is undersized for 55w current. LEDs run a lot cooler and pull less power, and don’t look yellow.
